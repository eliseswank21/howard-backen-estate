Heritage & Design

An Architectural Masterpiece on Pritchard Hill

Perched atop 500+ private acres on Napa Valley's coveted Pritchard Hill, The Howard Backen Estate was conceived, designed, and built by legendary architect Howard Backen—widely celebrated as one of the most influential visionaries in modern Wine Country architecture.

Featuring interior design by Nicole Hollis and landscape architecture by Mark Bower, the property embodies Backen’s signature philosophy of creating structures in profound harmony with their natural surroundings. The architectural vocabulary is expressed through volcanic rock quarried directly on-site, dramatic cathedral timber ceilings, expansive retractable glass walls, proscenium-style archways, and a chef-inspired farmhouse kitchen with an outdoor wood-fired pizza oven—dissolving the boundary between interior grandeur and the breathtaking landscape.

The Enclave

Why Pritchard Hill

Pritchard Hill stands alone as one of Napa Valley's most prestigious, coveted, and geographically dramatic addresses. Hidden above the fog line on the eastern ridges of the valley, it is home to an extraordinary concentration of California's most legendary cult wineries and storied private vineyards.

From its commanding elevated vantage point, the estate offers unobstructed panoramic views across the Mayacamas Mountains, Rector Ridge, Rector Reservoir, and the Napa Valley floor below. Access here is inherently discreet and relationship-driven—granting residents and their guests an intimate sanctuary far removed from the public valley floor, yet connected to the very pinnacle of Napa winemaking.

Designed as an immersive on-property wellness sanctuary, an entire day can unfold effortlessly without leaving the grounds. Architecture & Design Immersion

For design connoisseurs, the estate itself offers a masterclass in California rustic-modern architecture. Guests experience how Howard Backen's massive sliding glass walls seamlessly integrate indoor timber pavilions with the surrounding mountain landscape. The organic palette of raw stone, blackened steel, and linen textures conceived by Nicole Hollis creates a deeply calming, museum-grade environment that inspires throughout every hour of the day.
